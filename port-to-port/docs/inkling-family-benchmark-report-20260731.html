Interpretation

This task taxes persistent procedural state: read tool semantics precisely, remember the objective, maintain cargo and port state, recover after explicit errors, retain the return route, and recognize when to stop. Small often navigates correctly—the path mean remains 13.7–14.4—but it does not reliably control the complete sequence.

The comparison is consistent with Thinking Machines’ own public results. Inkling Small stays close on many static evaluations but trails the larger model on Terminal Bench 2.1 (52.7% versus 63.8%) and Tau 3 Banking (13.6% versus 23.7%), both relatively stateful agentic tasks. Those results support the diagnosis; they do not prove that parameter count alone causes it. Thinking Machines’ Inkling report

The defensible conclusion is narrow: under these Baseten settings and this harness, Inkling Small has a long-horizon control gap relative to larger Inkling. Checkpoint maturity, preview FP8 serving, and the undocumented mapping to numeric effort=0.99 remain plausible contributors.
